About This Project

Objective

PlantCare AI is an EDP (Experiential/Entrepreneurial Development Program) AI/ML internship project. It uses computer vision to identify plant diseases from leaf images and provide actionable, general information about symptoms and management.

Dataset

The model is trained on a PlantVillage-style leaf-image dataset, organized by plant and disease class, cleaned to remove corrupt/duplicate images before a stratified train/validation/test split.

Model

Two models are built and compared: a custom convolutional neural network (CNN) baseline, and a transfer-learning model built on EfficientNetB0 (fine-tuned on the dataset). The better-performing model, based on real held-out test metrics, is used for predictions in this app.

Evaluation

Accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score are calculated per class on a held-out test set, with a confusion matrix and error analysis to identify commonly confused diseases.
