How it works

The AI technology

PlantCare AI is built on a deep convolutional neural network, using transfer learning from EfficientNetB0, trained on a cleaned and stratified split of a PlantVillage-style leaf image dataset. Every prediction includes a confidence score, and low-confidence predictions are clearly flagged as uncertain rather than guessed.
